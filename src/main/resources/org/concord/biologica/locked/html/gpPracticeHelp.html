This Geno-Pheno practice session provides you with a female and a male organism (or in the case of the pea and arabidopsis organisms, Plant One and Plant Two) and their respective chromosomes.

Each of the allele labels on the chromosomes is a pull down menu. Click and hold your left mouse button on an allele label to make the menu appear. Move the arrow to highlight your choice and release the mouse button to make the change. Use the pulldown menus on the chromosomes to change the organism's genotype or its collection of genes.

Notice that the organism's phenotype, or its physical appearance, changes as you change the alleles on the chromosomes. Use this Geno-Pheno practice session to explore. And feel free to make as many changes to the organisms as you like -- none of the things that you do will be saved or carried over into the lesson that you are currently working on.
